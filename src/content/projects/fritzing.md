### Electronics Prototyping for Everyone
Fritzing is the standard software tool for hobbyists, educators, and engineers prototyping microcontrollers (Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32). Led the concept exploration for Fritzing 2.0 and the component Parts Editor, transforming complex PCB routing and footprint creation into an intuitive, accessible experience.
