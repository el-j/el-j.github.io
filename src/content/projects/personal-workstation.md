### The Problem
Modern web developer portfolios have converged into repetitive, generic AI-generated templates: purple blur circles, cookie-cutter bento boxes, and meaningless repo dumps. There is no personality, no craft, and no reflection of what a senior fullstack engineer and UX designer actually cares about: **code ergonomics, systems architecture, and tactile interactive delight**.

### The Solution
A bespoke, retro-futuristic **Cyber-Tactile CRT Workstation** modeled after real hardware labs (vintage CRT monitors, Teenage Engineering grooveboxes, and Unix terminals).

### Key Architectural Decisions
- **Zero-JS Core**: Leveraging Astro 5 static site generation to produce instantaneous load times without hydration lag.
- **Islands of Interactivity**:
  - Live interactive terminal REPL with tab-completion.
  - Web Audio synthesizer with polyphonic oscillators and canvas oscilloscope.
  - Real-time token-to-AST interactive visualizer.
- **Accessibility & Motion First**: Automatic degradation to flat high-contrast mode when `prefers-reduced-motion` is active.
